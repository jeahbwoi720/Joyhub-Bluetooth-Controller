## 🌊 What's New in v1.1.0

### 🔘 Multi-Channel Waveform & Pulse Generator
* **Target Channel Selection**: Choose exactly which motor channels (Ch 1, Ch 2, Ch 3, Ch 4) receive the automated pulse wave with quick **All**, **Ch 1-2**, and **Clear** presets.
* **Selectable Waveform Shapes**:
  * 🌊 **Sine**: Classic smooth harmonic wave
  * 🔺 **Triangle**: Linear ramp up / ramp down
  * ⏹️ **Square**: Sharp ON / OFF burst pulses
  * 📈 **Sawtooth**: Gradual build-up with instant drop
  * 💓 **Heartbeat**: Rhythmic double-thump pulse with resting intervals
* **Per-Channel Speed Routing**: Dynamically modulates only selected channels without interfering with other motors or features.
